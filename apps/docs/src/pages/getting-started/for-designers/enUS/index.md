# Getting Started as Designers

Powerup your work with GDS and focus on providing the best experience to your users

## GDS library for designers

All you need to use GDS is Figma. The documentation on the design foundation, each of the components along with their variations, behavior, usage guidelines, etc is all available on this portal. And we have pre-define interface patterns coming soon.

## Accessing GDS Figma

In Figma, the GDS library is available under the “Banking Design” Team. If you’re already a part of the Banking Design team, you’ll be able to assess the <a href="https://www.figma.com/files/904079498882832935/project/36384948?fuid=1006506139549554171" target="_blank">GDS 2.0 project</a> on Figma by default.

The GDS library in Figma is currently available in a single file, “**GDS 2.0.0 - Foundation & Components**”. The interface patterns will be coming soon :) And the GDS components can be searched in the Assets Panel in your project files. To cross-check this, you can visit the ‘Libraries’ section in the ‘Assets’ section of Figma and ensure “GDS 2.0.0 - Foundation & Components’ is visible.

## Understanding the “Design” of GDS

To learn what drives our user experience and make informed product design decisions understanding GDS is crucial. Get started by familiarizing yourself with the contents of our library presented on this portal. The [Foundation](/en-US/os-theme/foundation/design-tokens/) and the [Components](/en-US/os-theme/components/button) section.

Zeta’s products cater to many types of markets, businesses, and users. GDS optimizes design and the designing process to cater to these several connected modular systems.

## Viewing changes in GDS

Any changes to GDS will be published with release notes, [version](/about/versioning/) update and logs.

When changes to GDS design tokens and components are made, they will not reflect in your Figma Assets until your Library is updated. We use different branches to make changes and merge to the main file. Branches are controlled environments—like sandboxes—where one can explore changes to styles, components, and other aspects without affecting the main file. 

Look out for the ‘Components Updates Available’ toast message in Figma to review the changes in GDS and update your existing library
